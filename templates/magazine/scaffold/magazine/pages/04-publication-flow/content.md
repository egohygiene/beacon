Edition metadata and page sources enter one validation boundary. Beacon checks
structure, rights declarations, reading order, accessibility metadata, print
geometry, and publication gates before rendering.

The digital PDF is optimized for review and download. The print PDF adds bleed,
trim boxes, crop marks, and signature checks. The web edition preserves
semantic text in a responsive reading surface.

> Consistency does not require every medium to look identical. It requires each
> medium to express the same reviewed edition honestly.
