## Current assumptions encoded in version 0.1.0

- Forms Version I applies to due dates on or after January 25, 2025.
- Specific Aims is generally limited to one page when the form includes it.
- The Research Strategy limit must be selected from the current table and then
  checked against the NOFO; Beacon does not guess it.
- NIH attachments use PDF, no larger than US Letter, with margins of at least
  one-half inch and body text of at least 11 points.
- Applicant-created headers and footers are omitted because NIH adds them when
  assembling the application.
- Biosketch/Common Forms and the 2026 DMS format remain official external
  artifacts rather than Beacon-authored substitutes.
