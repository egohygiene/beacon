# Readiness Checklist

`make check` automates structural checks, but submission readiness remains a
human and institutional decision.

## Concept gate

- [ ] The health problem, critical barrier, central premise, and expected
  impact can each be stated plainly.
- [ ] Every aim has a measurable outcome and a credible alternative strategy.
- [ ] The relationship between Specific Aims and the Research Strategy is
  one-to-one and internally consistent.
- [ ] Claims are supported by current citations and distinguish evidence from
  proposal language.

## Mechanism and institute gate

- [ ] Activity code and current NOFO are recorded in `proposal.toml`.
- [ ] Eligibility, clinical-trial designation, and due date are confirmed.
- [ ] NIMH program fit has been discussed with the relevant Program Officer.
- [ ] The current NOFO and related notices have been checked for deviations
  from general page limits or content instructions.

## Compliance gate

- [ ] Research Strategy page limit is recorded from current official sources.
- [ ] Human-subjects, inclusion, clinical-trial, vertebrate-animal, select-agent,
  genomic-data, and resource-authentication applicability is classified.
- [ ] The required 2026 DMS Plan format is used when the policy applies.
- [ ] Common Forms are prepared and certified through SciENcv.
- [ ] Hyperlinks and URLs appear only where current instructions or the NOFO
  explicitly allow them.
- [ ] Every final attachment has been inspected at 100% zoom after conversion.

## Submission gate

- [ ] Applicant organization and authorized submission contact are recorded.
- [ ] Institutional budget, grants-management, compliance, and routing reviews
  are complete.
- [ ] All `\TODO{}` markers and sample bibliography entries are removed.
- [ ] `stage = "submission-ready"` and `make check` completes without warnings
  or errors.
- [ ] The assembled application image is reviewed in the official submission
  system and again in eRA Commons.
